
The primary objective is to support data-driven decision-making by identifying boroughs that should be prioritised for targeted public health interventions.

---

## Key Questions
- Which London boroughs have the highest childhood obesity rates?
- How does childhood obesity relate to life expectancy and smoking prevalence?
- Which boroughs consistently underperform across multiple health indicators?
- Where should public health campaigns be focused to maximise impact?

---

## Data Sources
The analysis uses publicly available datasets related to London boroughs, including:
- Childhood obesity prevalence
- Male and female life expectancy
- Smoking prevalence
- Life satisfaction and happiness scores
- Rates of children being looked after
- Borough classification (Inner vs Outer London)

---

## Tools & Techniques
- **Power BI** – dashboard development and visual storytelling  
- **Power Query** – data cleaning, transformation, and standardisation  
- **DAX** – KPI and metric calculations  
- **Data Modelling** – star schema design with borough-level dimensions  
- **Visual Analytics** – KPI cards, ranked bar charts, scatter plots, and filters  

---

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards summarising key London-wide metrics  
- Top and bottom borough rankings by obesity and social care indicators  
- Scatter plots exploring relationships between obesity, life expectancy, and smoking  
- Filters for Inner vs Outer London and individual borough selection  
- Comparative views highlighting health inequalities across regions
